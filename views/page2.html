Problematic Use



Problematic social media use in teens involves compulsive scrolling, difficulty controlling time online, and withdrawal-like irritability when offline, often disrupting sleep, schoolwork, and real-life relationships.

Research links these patterns to higher risks of anxiety, depression, body-image concerns, and even suicidal thoughts, especially with passive nighttime use or cyber bullying exposure.

The World Health Organization notes over 1 in 10 adolescents show such problematic behaviors, highlighting a growing public health concern.
